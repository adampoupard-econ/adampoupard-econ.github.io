Research

Research and publications by Adam Poupard on ecological economics, biodiversity, food systems, political economy, and macroeconomics.

My research lies at the intersection of ecological economics, political economy, biodiversity policy, and food systems. Links below point to the official publication record; language-specific PDFs are shown when they are publicly available.

Les systèmes nationaux de protection sociale alimentaire. Comparaison structurale France-Brésil

Adam Poupard (2026). EconomiX Working Papers, 2026-9.

A structural comparison of food social-protection systems in France and Brazil and of the political alliances that shaped them.

Official publication · PDF — Français · PDF — Português (Brasil)

Who pays for a protected Earth? Unequal economic exposure in the race to GBF Target 3

Adam Poupard and Gabriel Santos Carneiro (2026). Land Use Policy, 168, 108096.

An analysis of how the economic exposure associated with the 30×30 biodiversity target is distributed across countries and global value chains.

Official publication · PDF — English

From Climate to Nature Macro-Criticality: Exploring New Scenarios and Modeling Frameworks

Jeffrey Althouse, Bastien Bedossa, Etienne Espagne, Léna Faucher, Morgane Gonon, Katie Kedward, Angela Modica Scala, Adam Poupard, and Romain Svartzman (2025). CEPR Discussion Paper, DP19987.

A review and extension of scenario and modeling approaches for identifying the macroeconomic and financial consequences of nature loss and nature-positive transitions.

Official publication

The economic and financial risks of implementing the ‘30x30’ Global Biodiversity Framework targets

Katie Kedward and Adam Poupard (2024). UCL Institute for Innovation and Public Purpose Working Paper Series, 2024/13.

A conceptual and empirical assessment of the macrofinancial risks arising from conservation-related land-use constraints.

Official publication · PDF — English

Macroéconomie des transitions énergétiques — Explorations dans le cas français par la mise en œuvre d’un modèle réduit

Frédéric Ghersi, Adam Poupard, and Julien Lefèvre (2024). CIRED Working Papers, hal-04384918.

A compact model used to compare how alternative economic paradigms shape assessments of the macroeconomic effects of the energy transition in France.

Official publication · PDF — Français

Global Carbon Accounts 2022

Marion Fetet, Sébastien Postic, and Adam Poupard (2022). Institute for Climate Economics (I4CE).

A global overview of explicit carbon-pricing mechanisms, their coverage, price levels, and the use of the revenues they generate.

Official publication · PDF — English
